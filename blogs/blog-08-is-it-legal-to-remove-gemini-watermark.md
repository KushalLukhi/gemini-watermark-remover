## What the Law Says About Watermark Removal

In intellectual property law (such as the United States Digital Millennium Copyright Act - DMCA Section 1202), removing "Copyright Management Information" (CMI) can be illegal if done to facilitate infringement of someone else’s copyrighted work.

However, in the context of your own AI generations:
1. **You Are the Prompt Creator:** You are not stealing another human creator's copyrighted art; you are editing media produced specifically for you by a generative model.
2. **AI-Generated Imagery Copyright Status:** In most jurisdictions (including rulings from the US Copyright Office), purely machine-generated visual outputs do not qualify for traditional copyright protection because they lack human authorship.
3. **No Deception / No Fraud:** Modifying visual media is lawful as long as it is not used to commit fraud, impersonate individuals without consent, or breach consumer protection regulations.
