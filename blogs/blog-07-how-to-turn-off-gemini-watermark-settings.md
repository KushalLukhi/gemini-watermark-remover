## Quick Answer (For Google Featured Snippet / AI Overviews)

> **Yes, you can turn off the visible watermark for future image generations in Google Gemini:**  
> 1. Go to **[gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)** and log into your Google account.  
> 2. Click the **Settings (gear icon)** in the navigation menu.  
> 3. Select **Media watermark**.  
> 4. Switch the toggle for visible watermark to **Off**.  
>  
> *Note:* This setting only disables watermarks on **new images generated after toggling the setting**. It does **not** retroactively remove watermarks from images or Veo videos you have already created or downloaded.

## Detailed Step-by-Step Guide: How to Disable Gemini Watermarks

Google recently introduced account-level controls allowing users to disable the visible 4-point sparkle logo on AI-generated images. Here is how to configure it:

### Step 1: Open Google Gemini
Open your web browser and navigate to **[gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)**. Ensure you are signed in with the Google account you use to generate images.

### Step 2: Locate the Settings Menu
Look at the bottom-left sidebar navigation or click your profile avatar in the upper right. Click on **Settings** (represented by a gear icon ⚙️).

### Step 3: Find "Media Watermark"
In the settings modal or dropdown, click on the **Media watermark** option.

### Step 4: Toggle Visible Watermarks Off
You will see a setting labeled *"Show visible watermark on generated media"*. Switch this toggle to **Off**.

From this point forward, images generated within your Gemini chat session will be delivered without the bottom-right sparkle badge.

## What the Google Settings Toggle CANNOT Do

While this native feature is welcome, it has several critical limitations:

1. **No Retroactive Removal:** The setting only applies to future creations. It cannot modify or clean the hundreds of images you already downloaded in the past.
2. **Does Not Work on Google Veo Videos:** Video clips generated with Google Veo and Omni Flash still include the visible watermark overlay by default.
3. **Third-Party API & Studio Generations:** If you generate media through third-party platforms, Google AI Studio, or Vertex AI APIs, watermarks may still be added automatically.
